# Generic Security Deposit Demand Letter Template

**IMPORTANT: This is a static template for unsupported jurisdictions. It is NOT generated per-user.**
**Legal review required before launch. Per PRD §7.9.**

---

**THIS IS A TEMPLATE, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
**Verify your state's security deposit laws before relying on this letter.**
**Consider consulting a licensed attorney for advice about your specific situation.**

---

[Your Name]
[Your Current Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
[Your Email Address]
[Your Phone Number]
[Date]

[Landlord/Property Management Company Name]
[Landlord's Address]
[City, State, ZIP]

**RE: Demand for Return of Security Deposit**
**Property Address: [Rental Property Address]**
**Lease Period: [Start Date] to [End Date]**
**Security Deposit Amount: $[Amount]**

Dear [Landlord Name / Property Manager],

I am writing to request the return of my security deposit in the amount of $[Amount] paid in connection with my tenancy at the above-referenced property.

My tenancy at this property ended on [Move-Out Date]. As of the date of this letter, I have not received the return of my security deposit [or: I have received only $[Partial Amount] of my $[Total Amount] security deposit].

I left the property in [good/clean/the same] condition as when I moved in, accounting for normal wear and tear. [If applicable: I provided my forwarding address to you on [Date] via [Method].]

I am requesting the full return of my security deposit in the amount of $[Amount to be returned]. Please send the deposit to my current address listed above.

[If deductions were made without itemization:]
I have not received an itemized statement of deductions as may be required by applicable state law. I request that you provide such a statement along with the return of any remaining deposit balance.

[If deductions were made with itemization:]
I have reviewed the itemized statement of deductions dated [Date]. I dispute the following deductions:

- [Deduction 1]: $[Amount] — [Brief reason for dispute, e.g., "This constitutes normal wear and tear"]
- [Deduction 2]: $[Amount] — [Brief reason for dispute]

Please return the disputed amount of $[Total Disputed Amount] to my current address.

I look forward to resolving this matter promptly. If I do not receive a response within [reasonable period, e.g., 14-30 days], I may pursue the remedies available to me under applicable law, which may include filing a complaint with the state attorney general or a claim in small claims court.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]

---

**INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE:**

1. Replace all bracketed items [like this] with your actual information.
2. Delete sections that don't apply to your situation (e.g., remove the itemization dispute section if your landlord didn't provide deductions).
3. Research your state's specific security deposit laws — deadlines, required notices, and penalties vary significantly by state.
4. Send via certified mail with return receipt requested so you have proof of delivery.
5. Keep a copy of everything you send.

**RESOURCES:**
- Your state's official deposit statute (check your state legislature's website)
- Your state's small claims court self-help portal
- LawHelp.org — find free legal aid in your area

**This template does not cite any specific state statute, does not reference state-specific deadlines, and does not include penalty language. It is a general-purpose starting point. For a letter grounded in your state's specific laws and deadlines, check back — we may add coverage for your state based on demand.**
